## Voice

- Source: The user specifies a warm, plain voice. No writing samples establish an observed house style. [U1]
- Practical choices: Use familiar words, short sentences, and a gentle invitation. Describe an ordinary use before introducing an abstract benefit. Avoid pressure or exaggerated enthusiasm.
- Use: notebook, list, sketch, everyday, a thought, a quick note.
- Avoid: revolutionary, unlock your creativity, ultimate, premium, guaranteed; the last three also imply unsupported product claims.
- Proposed original rewrite 1: “Optimize your daily ideation” → “A place for your everyday lists and sketches.”
- Proposed original rewrite 2: “Capture inspiration without limits” → “Have a thought? Give it a little space.”
- Both examples are proposed copy, not quotes from existing brand writing.
