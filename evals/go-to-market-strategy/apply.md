# Go To Market Strategy: apply

## Brief

ArcSeal is a fictional regional industrial sealing service launching a documented inspection-and-test offer. Buyers can repair internally or purchase replacement seals. Two engineers can serve four jobs per week. Pilot budget $2,000; no measured acquisition costs or savings. No regional expansion commitment.

## Request

Recommend a focused starting segment, route to market, sales motion, and bounded pilot.

## Expected behavior

Use available context without repeating onboarding. Choose a feasible route and buyer situation with tradeoffs, qualification, capacity guardrails, and hypotheses; do not confuse a GTM strategy with a channel list.

Score the actual output with [the rubric](../rubric.md). A structural fixture pass is not a behavioral pass.
