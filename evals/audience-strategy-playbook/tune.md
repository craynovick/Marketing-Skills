# Audience Strategy Playbook: tune

## Brief

Mend Studio offers in-person clothing repair in one city. It can handle 25 jobs weekly. Interviews: commuters want predictable pickup; vintage shoppers want careful repairs. Five recent jobs came from each group. No margins or acquisition data. Budget is $300 for a pilot. The prior artifact follows the Apply fixture; use it as the canonical draft.

## Request

The founder confirms commuters remain the priority. New timing logs show complex vintage repairs take three times longer. Propose changes without rewriting confirmed service facts.

## Expected behavior

Produce the full revised artifact when appropriate, preserving unaffected content and confirmed facts. Separate new evidence from proposed shared-context changes. Compare urgency, reachability, serviceability and unknown economics; use individual buying situations, not enterprise committees.

Score the actual output with [the rubric](../rubric.md). A structural fixture pass is not a behavioral pass.
