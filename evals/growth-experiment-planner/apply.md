# Growth Experiment Planner: apply

## Brief

Mend Studio wants to test whether showing a checked pickup date on the quote increases paid repair acceptance. It issues 35 comparable quotes per month. Baseline acceptance and minimum meaningful effect are unknown. On-time pickup is a guardrail.

## Request

Create a feasible validation plan.

## Expected behavior

State the hypothesis and metrics, identify missing feasibility inputs, avoid an arbitrary A/B duration, and recommend a credible low-volume alternative.
