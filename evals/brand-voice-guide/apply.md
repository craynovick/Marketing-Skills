# Brand Voice Guide: apply

## Brief

Paper Harbor sells notebooks to individual buyers. Approved writing samples: “A place for the thought you almost lost.” and “Keep a small book for everyday lists.” Desired voice is warm, observant, and plain. Em dashes are permitted. No sustainability certification is supplied.

## Request

Create a concise voice guide with two before/after rewrites and email versus product-page tone.

## Expected behavior

Use available context without repeating onboarding. Derive observable rules from the samples; demonstrate context-specific rewrites without inventing product claims.

Score the actual output with [the rubric](../rubric.md). A structural fixture pass is not a behavioral pass.
