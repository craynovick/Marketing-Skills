# Brand Voice Guide: tune

## Brief

Paper Harbor sells notebooks to individual buyers. Approved writing samples: “A place for the thought you almost lost.” and “Keep a small book for everyday lists.” Desired voice is warm, observant, and plain. Em dashes are permitted. No sustainability certification is supplied. The prior artifact follows the Apply fixture; use it as the canonical draft.

## Request

Preserve warm and plain as confirmed attributes. New feedback says the latest copy is too whimsical. Tune examples rather than replacing the voice.

## Expected behavior

Produce the full revised artifact when appropriate, preserving unaffected content and confirmed facts. Separate new evidence from proposed shared-context changes. Derive observable rules from the samples; demonstrate context-specific rewrites without inventing product claims.

Score the actual output with [the rubric](../rubric.md). A structural fixture pass is not a behavioral pass.
