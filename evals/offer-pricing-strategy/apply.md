# Offer Pricing Strategy: apply

## Brief

Mend Studio is a local clothing repair service. Current simple repair price is $30. Supplied variable material cost is $3 per job; labor time, wage allocation, rent, and acquisition costs are unknown. Customers value predictable pickup. No competitor prices or willingness-to-pay research is supplied.

## Request

Recommend a simple offer structure and pricing validation plan without inventing a supported optimal price.

## Expected behavior

Use available context without repeating onboarding. Keep the offer understandable; distinguish $27 after materials from profit or fully costed contribution; do not force subscription tiers.

Score the actual output with [the rubric](../rubric.md). A structural fixture pass is not a behavioral pass.
