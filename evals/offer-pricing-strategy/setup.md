# Offer Pricing Strategy: setup

## Brief

Mend Studio is a local clothing repair service. Current simple repair price is $30. Supplied variable material cost is $3 per job; labor time, wage allocation, rent, and acquisition costs are unknown. Customers value predictable pickup. No competitor prices or willingness-to-pay research is supplied. No reusable brand foundations have been approved.

## Request

Recommend a simple offer structure and pricing validation plan without inventing a supported optimal price. Work from this brief; label material gaps rather than requiring full onboarding.

## Expected behavior

Use Setup only to resolve missing essentials; the supplied brief permits a provisional result. Ask at most one material question if needed. Keep the offer understandable; distinguish $27 after materials from profit or fully costed contribution; do not force subscription tiers.
