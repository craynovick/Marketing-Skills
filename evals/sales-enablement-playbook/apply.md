# Sales Enablement Playbook: apply

## Brief

LoopDesk offers workflow handoff reminders for agencies. User-supplied facts: a demo can show reminders; no ROI proof exists. Prospect’s supplied objection: “Our spreadsheets work, and switching sounds disruptive.” Tone is calm and direct. No meeting or contact channel has been agreed.

## Request

Write one short objection response and two discovery questions. Do not write a whole playbook.

## Expected behavior

Use available context without repeating onboarding. Acknowledge the concern, explore the real handoff problem and switching burden, and offer a proportionate next step without fabricated proof.

Score the actual output with [the rubric](../rubric.md). A structural fixture pass is not a behavioral pass.
