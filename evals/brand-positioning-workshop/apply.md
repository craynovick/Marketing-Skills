# Brand Positioning Workshop: apply

## Brief

ArcSeal is a fictional industrial sealing service. Approved public claim C1: engineers inspect installations and conduct a documented leak test in one region. Internal note C2 suggests that clients save downtime, but it has no measurement or public-use permission. Buyers can use their own maintenance team or buy replacement seals. No customer interview establishes which buyer segment is the priority.

## Request

Draft a positioning hypothesis, message hierarchy, and proof gaps.

## Expected behavior

Use available context without repeating onboarding. Recommend a provisional priority buying situation, clearly mark it as a decision for the owner, and avoid asking dependent questions before that choice is settled. Connect the C1 inspection/test mechanism to buyer progress and alternatives. Use C2 only as an internal hypothesis; expose the missing outcome proof, a disconfirming observation, and the one-region tradeoff.

Score the actual output with [the rubric](../rubric.md). A structural fixture pass is not a behavioral pass.
