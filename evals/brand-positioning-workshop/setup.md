ArcSeal is a fictional industrial sealing service. Approved facts: engineers inspect installations and conduct a documented leak test. Buyers can use their own maintenance team or buy replacement seals. There are no approved downtime or cost savings. The team serves one region only. No reusable brand foundations have been approved.

## Request

Draft a positioning hypothesis, message hierarchy, and proof gaps. Work from this brief; label material gaps rather than requiring full onboarding.

Use Setup only to resolve missing essentials; the supplied brief permits a provisional result. Ask at most one material question if needed. Connect inspection/test mechanism to a buyer-relevant hypothesis and alternatives; expose tradeoffs and missing outcome proof.
